The Apothecary Diaries

Scroll ↓

Sold into the rear palace, she counts the days by what grows in the yard.

Maomao was an apothecary's daughter before she was anything else. Kidnapped and put to work as a low-ranking servant behind the palace walls, she keeps her head down until a string of unexplained illnesses among the emperor's infant children gives her a reason to notice everything.

She never meant to be seen. Knowledge has a way of surfacing anyway.

A eunuch official notices the girl who reads poison like handwriting.

Jinshi, elegant and irritatingly perceptive, starts finding reasons to call on her. Maomao would rather be left to her herbs — but the cases keep finding her, one strange symptom at a time.
